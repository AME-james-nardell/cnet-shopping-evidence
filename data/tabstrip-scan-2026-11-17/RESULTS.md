**Conclusion (corrected after the independent review).** Five recordings where Compare fired show a separate icon-only tab opening at the far left of the tab strip and closing 7 to 17 seconds later: attempts 8, 9 and 10, the 17 Nov control, and attempt 11 (the oven, which has no usable HAR). A sixth, attempt 6, shows the opening only. Where a HAR covers it, the open time is consistent with the background page's network activity (requests from the landing page end, several with status 0, just before the visible close). In the four negatives (Target, Macy's attempt 13, attempt 7, the 17 Nov stand-down) no additional icon-only tab appears at the tested interaction while the browser is visible; ordinary setup tabs do appear in two of them. The three unexplained tabs (attempt 1, the 19:32 run, the early Home Depot run) have no adequate network record. The code opens inactive pinned tabs on other paths too (coupon and seller clicks with a background URL, cashback), so they aren't counted. Video can't show the tab's pinned property or ID; the attribution to Compare rests on the video, the HAR's `origin=aff_savings` redirect and the Compare handler agreeing.
